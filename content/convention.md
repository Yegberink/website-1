title: Convention
---

The Modelblocks convention describes how a module should be structured to permit interoperability with the Modelblocks ecosystem.

It consists of two parts:

- The [documentation]({{< param docs_url >}}) describing how components should be structured and what file and metadata conventions to follow.
- The [data module template]({{< param github_url >}}/data-module-template/) with which new modules following the convention can be created.

The convention is versioned and its current major version is {{< badge convention 1 >}}.
